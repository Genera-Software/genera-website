🐾 A letter from Duncan

This time last year, our system was held together with tape.

I want to write this one myself. Genera didn't start as new software built from nothing. It started as the system we built to run Duncan's Dog Co ourselves. A year ago, that system handled the basics: booking a dog into daycare, keeping a record of who's who. It didn't do much beyond that. If you run boarding, or grooming, or you're a dog walker who looked at us back then and thought "this isn't for me," you were right, it wasn't ready for you yet.

Then Dihan came on board. That's the one thing that changed everything else

I've known Dihan for thirty years. He designed the very first business card we ever had at Duncan's Dog Co. He's been around us and the dogs ever since.

He'd just finished a project he'd been on for over a decade, building delivery software for restaurants. Drivers, routes, timings, everything having to land in the right order. His skill set was exactly what the doctor ordered.

We'd had good people on it before him but never quite found its rhythm and progress was slow. Now something lands most weeks. A tweak, an amendment, or a whole new feature. We all get so excited when a message from him comes through. His work is at dihan.com.

The other thing that changed it is you.

Our founding community told us exactly how they run things day to day, and we built to that.
This was Genera. circa 2018

You shouldn't need a second app for anything.

Daycare, walking, boarding, grooming, kennels. One login.
This is Genera now.

If you're a walker, a groomer, or you board dogs

If you signed up a while back and never really used us, this bit is for you specifically. A walk gets its own start and end time now, not a whole day sat next to a full day of daycare. A grooming appointment holds its own slot, the same way a walk does. A boarding stay is counted in nights, with one page showing every arrival and departure across the month, whether you call it boarding or kennels.

Some of you reading this are already using Genera every day, and you were there for the bare bones version of it. Thank you for sticking with us while we built it properly. A lot of what is in this email exists because you asked for it.

And some of you signed up, had a look, and never came back. If that's you, I honestly don't blame you. It wasn't ready. It is now.

So this is me asking you to give us another go. Log back in and have a proper look around. It is not the same system you left.

Duncan
